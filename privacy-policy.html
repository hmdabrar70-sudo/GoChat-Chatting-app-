Privacy Policy — Butterfly Messenger Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Butterfly Messenger (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Butterfly Messenger. Butterfly Messenger is connected to the GoChat platform. Some account information and communication features may therefore be shared between Butterfly Messenger and GoChat. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 1. Information We Collect Depending on how you use the App, we may collect or process the following information: A. Account Information - Email address used for registration and authentication. - Display name and username. - Profile photo and other profile information you choose to provide. - Account identifiers generated by Firebase Authentication. B. Messages and Communication Data - Text messages exchanged with other users. - Message timestamps and conversation-related information. - Message reactions and replies. - Voice messages and other content you send through the App. - Group conversation information, where applicable. C. Photos, Videos, and Stories When you upload content, we may process: - Profile photos and other images. - Videos and voice-message media. - Stories and story-related information. - Media URLs and associated timestamps or expiration information. D. App Preferences and Local Data The App may store certain preferences and application-related information locally on your device, such as theme settings and other interface preferences. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the information described above to: - Create and manage user accounts. - Authenticate users and maintain account sessions. - Send and receive messages. - Display profiles, conversations, stories, and shared media. - Support reactions, replies, blocking, and other communication features. - Synchronize relevant account and conversation information between GoChat and Butterfly Messenger. - Maintain the security, reliability, and functionality of the App. - Respond to support requests and investigate reported problems. 3. Third-Party Services Butterfly Messenger uses third-party services to provide its functionality. Firebase by Google We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to support account authentication and store application data, including relevant profile, conversation, and communication information. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Cloudinary We use Cloudinary to upload and deliver certain media files, including images, videos, and voice-message media. Cloudinary Privacy Policy: https://cloudinary.com/privacy These third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms. 4. How Information Is Shared We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared in the following circumstances: - With other users: Information and content you choose to share may be visible to the intended recipients or other users, depending on the relevant feature and its settings. - Between GoChat and Butterfly Messenger: Relevant account and communication data may be synchronized because both applications use the same platform infrastructure. - With service providers: Information may be processed by Firebase, Cloudinary, and other providers necessary to operate the App. - For legal and safety reasons: Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the App, or the rights and safety of others. 5. We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their login credentials confidential and avoid sharing sensitive personal information through messages. 6.
